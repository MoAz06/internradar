## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply only through the job ad form; applications “sent by email, direct messages, or any other channel than our application forms, will not be accepted”.
- Nordea's general process: “You will attend one to three interviews”, meeting your future leader, someone from HR or others relevant to the role.
- For some positions there are work-related assessments such as ability tests, and “you will always receive feedback on your test results”.
- After interviews and assessments Nordea runs background checks on the final candidates. For candidates in Finland the selected person also gets a security clearance.
- Nordea says CV screening is done by people: “We review all applications manually”.
