## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply for a role on the Career Site. If your application is positive, you meet HR staff and managers of the team (“incontrerai il personale HR e i manager della struttura interessata”).
- The FAQ says selection has two steps: an interview with the Recruiting, Coaching & Assessment Center on your soft skills, and one with the team where you could work.
- Some Students & Graduates selections end with a Contest Day of recruiting games, interviews and activities. The bank says “Al termine di ogni step di selezione riceverai sempre un feedback” (you always get feedback after each step).
- Internships are mostly “extracurriculari e retribuiti” (extracurricular and paid), last at most 6 months, and are full time.
