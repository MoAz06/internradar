## Process, as the company describes it

- Summer internship: you “complete the online application form” and attach your CV, diplomas and recent transcripts.
- Next comes an online assessment of “personality and learnability tests”, which “will take approximately 60 mins”.
- Then a self-recorded video interview that “will take approximately 20 minutes to complete”. Both the test and the video are due within 48 hours of the invite.
- Offers go out as soon as possible, “typically in December”.
- The FAQ lists the steps as online application, online assessment, video interview, an assessment period, offer or rejection, and a background check if applicable.
- You can pick at most two disciplines per country across all your applications.

## Re-apply rule

If you are turned down, Equinor says you are “very welcome to apply again at a later stage”. No waiting period is stated.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: not stated for interns. For graduates the FAQ lists “Competitive salaries” and a bonus scheme, no figure.
- Visa: Equinor “does not support, or sponsor work or visa permits for internship applications”.
- Relocation: not mentioned.
