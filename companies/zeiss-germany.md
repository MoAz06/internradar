## Process, as the company describes it

- After you apply, the recruiting team checks all documents. If you fit the profile, you are invited to a first interview.
- In a further interview you also meet the hiring managers. After all interviews you get an offer letter.
- For student roles ZEISS advises applying 3 to 6 months before your preferred start; the start date is then agreed with the team.
- Student applications need a CV and certificates or a transcript; an enrolment certificate and cover letter are optional. If you are not from the EU, you upload a valid residence permit and a work permit.
- ZEISS does not take speculative applications; you apply to advertised roles only.

## Re-apply rule

You can apply to as many roles as you like with the same candidate profile. After you withdraw an application you cannot apply again to that role with the same profile; the recruiting team has to reactivate it.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Students get “eine attraktive Vergütung” that depends on location and role; no figure given.
- Travel costs for an in-person interview can be claimed back.
- Not stated: relocation support or visa sponsorship.

## Notes

- ZEISS takes both voluntary internships and mandatory internships (Pflichtpraktikum).
- You must be enrolled and not yet hold a master's degree; after a bachelor's degree a voluntary internship is still possible.
