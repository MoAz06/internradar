## Process, as the company describes it

- Stage 1: you “complete the application form and upload your CV”, then get “a questionnaire to ask a few supplementary questions” by email. Security verification starts here.
- Stage 2, for graduates and industrial placements: “a one way video interview on some competency-based questions”, four recorded videos.
- Stage 3: an interview, virtual unless you are local to the site. The FAQ: “These are the only stages of the recruitment process.” Summer placements skip the video stage.
- Stage 4: an offer; “we will provide all candidates with feedback”.
- Apply early: roles “are screened on a first come first serve basis. As a result, applications will be closed when each role is filled.”
- Graduates: “The assessment process will begin in January and continue until all roles have been filled”, and it “can take until August to complete.” Graduates usually start at the beginning of September.
- You can apply for more than one discipline or role.

## Re-apply rule

Not stated for graduates, placements or summer roles. For apprenticeships Leonardo UK says: “If I am unsuccessful can I apply again next year? Yes, you can apply any year.”

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: graduates get “a starting salary of £34,000 per annum, plus a £1,000 welcome bonus”, and a £1,000 exit bonus when they finish the programme.
- The Edinburgh industrial placement gives “a salary of £26,228” and the Edinburgh summer placement “(£25,000)”, both without naming the period. Other placement postings give no figure.
- Housing: graduates “may be eligible for a Temporary Accommodation Allowance (TAA), subject to specific criteria.”
- Visa: “Unfortunately we are unable to sponsor visa applications for any early careers vacancies.” You must be eligible for UK Security Clearance, normally SC, which “includes having a minimum of 5 years' permanent residency in the UK.”

## Notes

- Graduate programmes last 12 months (Cyber & Security, Business Support), 18 months (Electronics engineering) or 24 months (Helicopters, Yeovil). Sites include Edinburgh, Luton, Newcastle, Basildon, Bristol, Southampton and Yeovil.
