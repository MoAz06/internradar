## Process, as the company describes it

- Jane Street has no fixed deadline: “our applications are reviewed on a rolling basis”.
- After applying, “you can expect to hear from a recruiter in just a few days.”
- One application is considered “for all open roles globally”, so you can be moved to another team or office.
- Software engineering: every path starts with “a technical interview that takes place over Zoom” and ends with final rounds “in person in one of our offices”, mostly coding problems.
- Machine learning engineer interns get the same steps as software interns plus one ML round: after the Zoom coding interview comes “an on-site interview with 2-4 technical rounds”.
- Quantitative research interviews are “a mix of the two” (trading and software engineering).
- You can interview in whatever language you know best; OCaml is not needed.
- After the interviews: “You should hear back from us within a week.”

## Re-apply rule

You can reinterview after a rejection. For current students Jane Street “usually recommend waiting to reapply for at least a year”, and says this is a guideline, not a hard rule.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: no figure on the London postings checked.
- Interview travel: they “take care of your travel and hotel costs”.
- Visa: “This changes from office to office, and depending on the role.” Ask your recruiter.
