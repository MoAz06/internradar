## Process, as the company describes it

- After you apply you get an automated invite to an online technical test in your discipline, with 45 to 75 minutes depending on the test, and you “must pass to reach the next round”.
- The recruitment team then reviews each CV and invites those moving forward to a screening call.
- Next is a 30 minute online technical interview with an experienced consultant.
- Then come two to three technical interviews on site with consultants, plus a tour of the campus.
- The final round is with one or more leaders of the team you would join, on motivation, team fit and your grasp of consultancy and commerce. For internships the final round is online.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: no figure on the Melbourn postings. The summer internship postings say “This is a paid internship”, with free lunch and free accommodation in Cambridge “(subject to availability)”.
- Graduate postings list a 10% employer pension contribution, an annual profit-related bonus, virtual shares and “Comprehensive relocation support (if applicable)”.
- Visa: TTP “can provide sponsorship for work visas” for permanent graduate roles if you meet the UK Home Office criteria, and says it can “support with interns on graduate visas”.
- Office: all staff work on site five days a week at the campus just south of Cambridge.

## Notes

- Open roles: Summer Internship in Software Engineering and in Cyber / AI (2027), Graduate Consultant in Software Engineering, Cyber / AI and Electronic Engineering (2027), and a Year In Industry Placement in Electronic Engineering.
- All postings ask that you are on track for at least a 2:1 in a related subject, with strong A-Levels or an equivalent such as the IB.
- Summer internships last at least 8 weeks, 10 recommended, starting July 2027. You must have finished at least 2 years of your degree when the internship or placement starts, so students at the start of second year can apply.
- Graduate roles are for final-year students (Bachelors, Master’s or PhD) and start September 2027. The Year In Industry Placement starts August/September 2027.
- Roles open in September and are filled on a rolling basis with no deadline. TTP says all interns are considered for graduate roles.
- Applying to more than one: TTP recommends picking the one you prefer and telling them in the process that you are open to both.
