## Process, as the company describes it

- Every programme asks for a CV and a motivation letter. The students FAQ says “everyone completes an online assessment (intelligence test and personality measurement)”.
- First interviews are online, the second interview is in person. Interview invites come by SMS and email from a virtual assistant called Bo.
- Business & IT traineeship: after the interview there is a selection day at the office with a pitch or business case, an interview with a manager and a chance to meet colleagues.
- Early Career Programs: after the assessment and interview “you’ll tackle a business challenge during the case day”. The IT & Engineering page adds that “A security investigation is part of the process.”
- Before you start there is a screening (Certificate of Conduct and references) that can take up to 10 days.
- The students FAQ says to expect a response “no later than 1 week after applying”. The general FAQ aims for about 5 weeks from application to offer.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. The general FAQ says “We certainly encourage you to apply again if you are interested in another vacancy.” Once accepted you cannot switch to another programme.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Business & IT traineeship: the page shows “€ 3.600,-” with no period stated.
- IT & Engineering and Data & Analytics Early Career Programs: “€ 4.007,80” on the programme pages. The DevOps posting says “€4,007.80 per month based on a 40-hour working week”.
- Salary in a programme is not negotiable. Extras on the DevOps posting: a thirteenth month, 8% holiday allowance, a 10% Employee Benefit Budget and EUR 1,400 development budget per year.
- Relocation: “Rabobank does not assist with relocation for junior positions.”
- Visa: Rabobank can help international students get a work permit, but only if you already live in the Netherlands. Global Mobility can request the 30% ruling.
