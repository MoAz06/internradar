## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on Talos's Ashby board with your resume. Applications are evaluated “on a rolling basis until the class is filled”, and Talos says applications sent by email to staff will not be reviewed.
- Talos's internship page lists four rounds: a recruiter screen where you talk through a project you care about, a team screen with “a short coding exercise”, a coding interview with “a Talos-designed coding challenge using the language of your choice”, and a final interview with a senior team member.
- Talos says the final round “isn’t a coding interview”, though there may be some technical discussion.
- To prepare, Talos suggests reviewing coding fundamentals, practising coding exercises and being ready to talk about past projects. The interview platform and timing are not stated.
