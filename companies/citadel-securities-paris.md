## Process, as the company describes it

- Round 1 is a “45- to 60-minute remote interview” on technical and behavioral skills. A CoderPad link is sent before it.
- Round 2 “is onsite, and usually consists of three to five 60-minute interviews”. Next steps follow within two weeks.
- In the final review, hiring managers across Citadel Securities teams decide on fit.
- If there is an offer, the recruiting team contacts you with the final details. With offers from two businesses, you choose which one to take.
