## Process, as the company describes it

For the Software Engineering Internship:

1. Application with a CV.
2. Technical at-home test.
3. Recruiter phone interview.
4. In-person assessment centre: a day of one-on-one interviews and group activities.

Time between steps is not stated. The grad programme pages do not describe the process.

After a programme: "we'll help you level up with an offer of a permanent role."
