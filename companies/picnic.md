## Process, as the company describes it

- The Tech Academy posting lists five steps: CV screening, phone screening, interviews, an assessment day, then the offer.
- The Analytics graduate posting adds an online test after CV screening and a closing interview after the assessment day.
- Picnic aims to answer applications in “around 5 working days”.
- From application to offer takes 4 to 6 weeks on average, depending on your availability and how fast you hand in your assignment.

## Re-apply rule

Picnic's FAQ says you can apply again, but it recommends waiting 1 year between applications.
