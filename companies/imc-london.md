## Process, as the company describes it

- IMC describes a five-step process: application, assessment and/or interview, a first-round interview, final-round interviews, then a decision.
- Assessments vary by role; “Some may be online while others may not.”
- The first round covers your motivation, experience and questions about the role.
- Final rounds “may consist of multiple interview stations”, each with different people.
- After the final round IMC aims to give feedback “within a couple of days”.
- You can apply to more than one role, but each has its own process and you must pick one in the end.
