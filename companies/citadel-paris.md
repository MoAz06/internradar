## Process, as the company describes it

- Round 1 is a “45- to 60-minute remote interview” on technical and behavioral skills. Citadel sends a CoderPad link before it.
- Round 2 “is onsite, and usually consists of three to five 60-minute interviews”. Citadel says it comes back with next steps within two weeks.
- In the final review, hiring managers across Citadel teams decide on fit. If several teams are interested, you choose the best fit with your recruiter.
- If there is an offer, the recruiting team contacts you with the final details.
