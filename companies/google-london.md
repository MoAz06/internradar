## Process, as the company describes it

- Before interviews you “may be asked to take the Google Hiring Assessment”, and possibly a coding exercise if the role needs it.
- You typically have “one or two shorter conversations over phone or video with your recruiter”.
- Interviews are “a panel of interviews, either over video or in person”, and AI tools are not permitted during interviews.
- The process typically takes “roughly 6-8 weeks” once interviews start.
- Internship postings ask for a CV and a transcript in English, and say the team and project are picked from your background.

## Re-apply rule

Google's hiring FAQ says you can reapply if you were not selected, but “Typically, we ask for folks to wait at least a year to gain additional experience before reapplying for the same type of role.”

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Not stated: pay.
- Visa: the SWE PhD and Product Deployment Engineer postings say applicants “will not be eligible for immigration sponsorship”.
- Visa: Student Researcher applicants “must hold valid work authorization or meet local immigration eligibility requirements”.
