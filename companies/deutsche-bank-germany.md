## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on the careers site with a short application form and the documents asked for.
- Next comes an online assessment, depending on the area you applied for.
- The student FAQ lists the online assessments as a “Situational Judgment Test, Behavioral Questionnaire and an Ability Test”, followed by a pre-recorded video interview.
- In the video interview you get two minutes to prepare each answer and two minutes to record it, with one attempt.
- For internships in Germany the next step is video or phone interviews with Deutsche Bank staff.
- For trainee and entry programmes in Germany the next step is a selection day (Auswahltag) that focuses on your motivation and competencies.
- The FAQ says they generally do not reject candidates until all positions are filled, so a reply can take a while.

## Re-apply rule

Deutsche Bank's student FAQ says that in most cases, if you are unsuccessful for one role, you cannot be considered for another in the same hiring year. It also asks you to apply to just one role in one country.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Internships: “Competitive salary”, no figure given.
- Graduate programme: “Unlimited employment contract, competitive salary”, a company pension scheme, a Deutschlandticket subsidy and JobRad; no figure given.
- Visa: the FAQ says Deutsche Bank works with immigration providers to sponsor candidates who are eligible for work visas in most countries, and all offers depend on getting work authorization.
- Relocation: not mentioned.
