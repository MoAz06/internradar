## Process, as the company describes it

- Step one is “either a digital (via HireVue) or telephone interview” on your interests and technical skills.
- Then more in-depth conversations with hiring managers and possible teammates on technical topics, problem solving and team fit.
- If successful, you get a formal offer with role, pay and benefits.
- Arm aims to review all applications no later than two weeks after the closing date.
- You may apply to several roles, but Arm advises picking one core discipline.
- Arm's Graduate Program lasts two years and graduates join as permanent staff from day one.

## Re-apply rule

Arm says you are welcome to re-apply next year if you were unsuccessful, and to show any new skills or experience.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: “a starting salary of £48,000 (Bachelor level) or £49,000 (Master level)” on the Engineering Pathways and Quality Analytics postings.
- Relocation: support for domestic moves; the Quality Analytics role also covers regional moves within Europe.
- Visa: “Visa sponsorship support is also available for candidates who require it.”
