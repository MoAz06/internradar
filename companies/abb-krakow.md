## Process, as the company describes it

- ABB Kraków keeps a student internship talent pool: you leave your CV and they contact you when a suitable internship comes up.
- ABB's general steps: possibly a video interview or assessment first, then “a series of interviews with HR and hiring managers”.
- After the interviews you get feedback; if that and the compliance checks are positive, you get an offer.

## Re-apply rule

If ABB can't match you to an internship within 6 months, your application is rejected; you are welcome to apply again.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: “Paid internship”, on a civil-law contract (umowa zlecenie) with an hourly rate; no figure given.
- Work: most teams work 2 days in the office and 3 from home.

## Notes
