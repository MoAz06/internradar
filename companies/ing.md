## Process, as the company describes it

- Tech Class: “Apply by submitting your CV, motivation letter, and academic grade lists.”
- Tech Class: “After an initial screening and an online technical assessment, selected candidates will be invited to a digital selection day.”
- Internships: you upload a CV and motivation letter. ING's general NL FAQ says the process usually has “two interview rounds”, sometimes with an assessment or case.
- ING's NL FAQ gives target times: application review within 5 business days, interviews “typically within a week”, outcome within 3 business days after the interviews.
- The Tech International Talent Programme (graduate track) has 4 steps: application with CV and motivation letter, online test, interview with HR and your future trainee manager, then a business panel interview.
