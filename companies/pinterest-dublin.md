## Process, as the company describes it

- Software Engineering Intern: a recruiter reviews your resume, then sends a CodeSignal skills assessment. With a passing score you get a 30 minute recruiter phone screen, then a one hour technical interview with an engineer. If you pass, a hiring committee reviews your file and makes the final offer decision.
- Machine Learning Intern: the same CodeSignal test and 30 minute phone screen come first. Then several hiring managers review your profile, and if one is interested you get a one hour technical interview with an engineer, followed by a 30 minute call with the interested hiring managers before the final decision.
- Pinterest says roles “typically open in August or September”, and “by the end of the season, all applicants will be notified”. Its AI page says that in a live interview you should “assume AI assistance is not permitted” unless told otherwise, while some set parts, like a take home task or a section of a live screen, invite you to use AI.

## Notes

- Two Dublin roles: Software Engineering Intern 2027 and Machine Learning Intern 2027. Both run May 10, 2027 to August 27, 2027, full time.
- Software Engineering: you must be “Currently enrolled in a Bachelor's degree program” in Computer Science or a related field.
- Machine Learning: you must be working towards a Master's or PhD in Computer Science, ML, NLP, Statistics, Information Sciences or a related field.
- Pinterest says its university roles require that you are currently enrolled in an accredited program.
