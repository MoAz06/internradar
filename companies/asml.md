## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply with a CV and motivation letter. ASML says it will tell you “the status of your application within two weeks”.
- If your CV is selected you record answers to a few set questions in a pre-recorded HireVue video interview.
- ASML reviews the video “within seven business days” and may invite you to an interview.
- “A few days after the interview” you hear if you got the internship.
- ASML recommends “applying at least three months before your desired internship start date”, and needs four weeks for pre-onboarding.
- You cannot send an open application; you apply to a listed assignment on the career portal.
- Postings ask for a cover letter with a clear motivation for that specific assignment.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. ASML's interview FAQ says that if your application shows as “closed” you were not selected, and it encourages you to find another matching job and apply again.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: EU students get a full-time monthly allowance, €600 for associate, bachelor's and master's students (€400 for mbo).
- Housing: EU students who live 80 km or more away and move within 30 km of ASML get €450 a month; you find the room yourself.
- Travel: ASML pays second-class public transport between home and work, unless you have a free weekday student travel product.
- Visa: not explained beyond paperwork. Non-EU students studying in the Netherlands sign a tripartite (Nuffic) agreement with their university. Postings say you must be located in the Netherlands for the internship.
