## Process, as the company describes it

- KPN says you hear back “Binnen 5 werkdagen” after applying.
- If there is a possible match, KPN calls you for an introduction.
- After the introduction there is an assessment.
- Then an online first interview, followed by a second and sometimes a third interview at a KPN location, with your potential manager and sometimes a colleague.
- If both sides agree, you get an offer that KPN goes through with you.
- For internships, KPN says you hear within 5 working days whether you are invited for an introduction, followed by more interviews or an assessment.
