## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on LSEG's Workday site with your CV. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis and LSEG says it “may close applications early”.
- For the engineering internship and graduate roles, the next steps are a Technical and Coding Assessment, an untimed Immersive Online Assessment, a video interview in a “strength-based-interview style”, and a half day assessment centre.
- LSEG's own page says the technical assessment “covers key areas such as software engineering, coding, data structures and other relevant technical skills”.
- The Quantitative Services intern posting uses a different order: Immersive Online Assessment, video interview, then a coding assessment, then the half day assessment centre. All assessment centres are run virtually.
- You may only make one application per cycle. If you apply more than once, “we will review your first application only”. LSEG says it gives an update after each stage, and you track your status in your Workday profile.

## Re-apply rule

One application per recruitment cycle of 12 months. LSEG says: “If your application is unsuccessful, you are welcome to reapply in a future recruitment cycle, provided you continue to meet the programme’s eligibility criteria.”

## Pay, relocation, visa
