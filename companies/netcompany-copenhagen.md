## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply through the link in the posting and upload your CV and a motivational letter.
- A call with a Talent Acquisition Consultant from HR about the company, the role and your expectations.
- Interviews: an “Introduction Interview”, an open conversation about your experience, and for technical roles a “Case-based interview” where you work through a practical case, often on a whiteboard.
- Netcompany says the case is about your reasoning: “A perfectly solved case is less important than showing your reasoning and approach.”
- Netcompany aims to give “constructive feedback after interviews”.
