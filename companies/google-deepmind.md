## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply on Google Careers with a CV and a transcript in English.
- Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis; you should finish before 13 December 2026.
- Hearing back “can take upwards of 90+ days”. No reply after three months likely means Google went with others.
- One application is considered for Student Researcher spots across Google's research teams, including DeepMind and Google Research.
- DeepMind's general hiring steps (not specific to students): a 30-minute recruiter call, two or three skills interviews, final interviews with team leads, then a decision.
