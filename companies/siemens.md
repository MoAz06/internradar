## Process, as the company describes it

- Siemens Graduate Program: “The process has three major steps, with slight country variations”.
- Step 1 is CV screening: “A careful review of your resume and supporting documents”.
- Step 2 is an initial interview that can be “in person, telephone or live meeting or as a video interview.” “In some countries we will also invite to an psychometric assessment.”
- Step 3 is “the assessment center (virtual or onsite)”.
- Graduate program postings ask for a motivation letter, CV and all certificates, including proof of international experience.
- Applications only through the job portal; applications by email are not accepted.
