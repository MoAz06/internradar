## Process, as the company describes it

- SDE internships start with an online test: “The SDE Online Assessment (OA) is the first step in the hiring process for our full-time and internship SDE roles.”
- For interns “The OA takes about 90 minutes to complete”: two coding problems in about 70 minutes, a work styles part of about 15 minutes and a short survey.
- If you move on, “Amazon will reach out directly”; the student FAQ describes a call with your recruiter and then a virtual loop with several people from the team.
- During the coding part you may use public online resources, but “Your browser usage is logged.”
- “On average, the process takes between three and six weeks.”
- Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and “You can apply and interview for a maximum of 10 roles at a time.” Amazon does not take cover letters.
