## Process, as the company describes it

- For internships you apply with your CV and cover letter through the “apply now” button on the posting.
- After applying “you will receive a confirmation by email”, and TNO “will arrange an appropriate internship agreement”.
- If you start an internship, TNO asks for a Certificate of Conduct (VOG).
- Defence-related internships need an AIVD security clearance; the screening takes “approximately 8 weeks”.
- Your university must confirm in writing that you are enrolled and that the internship is part of your study programme.
- Interns must have a registered home address in the Netherlands at the start; working from abroad is not possible.
- For the traineeship you send a CV, a motivation letter and an assignment about your impact and learning goals.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: “a monthly internship allowance of € 615 for a full-time internship”.
- Travel: a contribution to travel costs if you have no student travel card.
- Traineeship: “a full-time position with a good salary”, no figure given.
- Visa: international interns need a BSN before the start; for the traineeship TNO “may be able to support you” with a residence and work permit.
