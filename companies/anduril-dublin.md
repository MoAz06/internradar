## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on the Greenhouse posting, and applications are reviewed “on a rolling basis”.
- The early careers page lists three parts: a “Recruiter Call”, “Technical and Behavioral Interviews” and “Role specific assessments”.
- The assessments “could include live coding challenges, presentations, or in depth technical discussions, depending on the team and role.”
- If there is a fit, a recruiter contacts you. If you hear nothing, Anduril has moved on with other candidates for that cohort.
- The number of rounds and the coding platform are not stated. By applying you agree to a third-party pre-employment risk and due diligence screening.

## Re-apply rule

If you do not hear back, the early careers page says “we highly encourage you to keep building your skills and apply again in the future.” No waiting period is stated.
