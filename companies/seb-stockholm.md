## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply to a specific trainee role. The application period for Sweden “will start on 16 November 2026” and the deadline is 10 January 2027, 23:59 CET. Write your CV in English.
- The recruitment process includes “Psychometric tests”, interviews “digitally and on-site”, and “A case exercise and presentation”.
- There is also an SEB Day, an event about SEB and the programme with former trainees.
- Final candidates go through background checks, for example “identity control, verification of qualifications, credit checks”. In some cases SEB also runs random drug checks.
- If it is a match you get a job offer and start in early September with onboarding in your department, then a joint kick-off with your cohort.
