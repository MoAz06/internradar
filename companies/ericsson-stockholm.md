## Process, as the company describes it

- Ericsson's general process: after you apply, “you will typically have one or two shorter conversations over the phone or video as initial contact”, usually with a recruiter.
- Then come interviews: “Depending on the role, you will have between one to three interviews.”
- A case interview or assessment is used for certain positions only.
- At the end Ericsson asks for references and contacts them, then makes an offer.
- Early Career Program roles: “The selection process includes an assessment, followed by a recruiter call and 2-3 interviews.” For some roles a case is added.
- The Early Career Program assessment is done once: “You only need to complete the assessment once”, even if you apply to several roles.
- Final step for the Early Career Program: meet senior leaders and stakeholders.
- “Around 100 candidates globally will be accepted into the Early Career Program.”
