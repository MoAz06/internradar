## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply through the form on the posting with an English CV; email applications are not accepted.
- If you go to the next round you “will be required to complete online assessments”, and by applying you confirm you can “take the assessment within 7 days”.
- Optiver aims to contact all candidates within 7 working days.
- Assessments can only be taken once every 8 months; old results are reused for a similar role.
- The FAQ mentions a first behavioural interview with a recruiter; later rounds are not described.
- Optiver expected 2027 campus applications to open “around the end of August 2026”.

## Re-apply rule

Optiver has a global re-apply policy for intern and graduate roles. If you completed an online assessment or interviewed for a similar role at any Optiver office in the past 8 months, you wait out those 8 months before applying again.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Software intern: “A highly competitive internship compensation package and accommodation included”, no figure given.
- Graduate QR (posting written for Amsterdam, also listed for London): performance-based bonus, 25 paid vacation days in year one, “Competitive relocation packages and visa sponsorship where necessary for expats”.
- Visa for the London software internship: Optiver “is unable to provide visa sponsorship” and you need the right to work in the UK for the whole internship.
