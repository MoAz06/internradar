## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply with “an updated CV or resume and a current unofficial or official transcript in English”.
- Google says “we review applications on a rolling basis” and asks you to apply early.
- Hearing back “can take upwards of 90+ days”. If you hear nothing in three months, the postings say Google likely went with other candidates for that role.
- For Student Researcher, you are “considered for Student Researcher positions across all of Google's research teams”, including DeepMind and Google Research.
