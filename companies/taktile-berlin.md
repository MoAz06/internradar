## Process, as the company describes it

- Taktile’s careers page lists four steps. First a call with a recruiter about your background and what you want next, then an interview with the hiring manager about your experience and how you solve problems.
- Next, “depending on the team”, a technical interview or a take-home challenge. Taktile says the goal “isn't to create a perfect solution” but to see how you work.
- The final interview is with a founder or senior leader about your values, motivation and ownership. If it goes well, the last step is a talk with your references.
- The intern postings ask for strong computer science basics: data structures, algorithms, networking, distributed systems and databases. They do not give a separate intern process or timeline.
- The postings say automated tools help screen applications, but no hiring decision is made by automated means alone.

## Re-apply rule

Taktile encourages you to “reapply in six months”. If you have recently been through its interviews, it usually recommends waiting “a few months” until you have new experience to share.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: no figure on the Berlin postings.
- Office: hybrid, 3 days a week at the Berlin HQ (or the Iasi engineering hub for the general intern role).
- Visa: support “is not guaranteed” and depends on the role and location. Taktile asks you to flag it early.
- Interns get a MacBook Pro.

## Notes

- Software Engineer Intern: Berlin or Iasi, Romania. Depending on the team you work on Python / FastAPI backend on AWS serverless, React/TypeScript front end, or both. The posting gives no duration or start date.
- Software Engineer Internship for HPI Students: only for students of the Hasso Plattner Institute. It is a 6 month full-time internship in Berlin starting April 2027.
- Fluent written and spoken English is required.
- Taktile recommends applying to at most three roles and telling the recruiter your top choice.
