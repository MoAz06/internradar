## Process, as the company describes it

- You submit an intern application and, if selected, a recruiter reaches out to learn about your background and skills.
- Figma says “Some roles require a portfolio review, take-home activity, or online assessment” and your recruiter shares the details.
- For software engineering you can expect “a language-agnostic coding interview focused on data structures and algorithms, plus a behavioral interview”.
- You also walk through your background with a potential mentor or manager.
- Cameras must stay on in video interviews.
- When you apply you pick the engineering areas you like most (Product, Platform/Infrastructure, Security Engineering or Open) so Figma can match you to a team.

## Re-apply rule

Figma says candidates “may not reapply to the same role while an application is active or within 90 days of receiving a rejection.” You may also send at most 5 applications in any rolling 30 day period.
