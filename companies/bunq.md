## Process, as the company describes it

- bunq describes a 7-step process. You start with a short form: “No CVs, no cover letters.” They reply within 5 working days.
- Then an online assessment on how you think and work, followed by a skills assessment “built around real scenarios relevant to the role”.
- A 30-minute recruiter call covers how bunq works and practical topics “like visas, potential start dates, and next steps.”
- A 30-minute call with your potential team lead.
- A “Get Shit Done Day” at the office with a role-specific assignment, then the offer.
- All new hires go through pre-employment screening by a third party.
