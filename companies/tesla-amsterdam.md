## Process, as the company describes it

- Tesla's intern FAQ says that if you are picked, “We will contact you for an interview.”
- Tesla says it “may not be able to contact every applicant” because of the number of applications.
- Tesla's interview guide says interviewers may ask “technical questions to better understand your experiences”, and for technical challenges “the interviewer may value a compelling thought process more than a correct answer”.
- Tesla “may recruit for up to four months after an initial posting” and closes postings “once each recruiting cycle has concluded”.

## Re-apply rule

Tesla's intern FAQ says “Eligible students may reapply to the same position for multiple terms.”

## Pay, relocation, visa

- “Interns receive competitive compensation”, no figure given.
- Visa: “All interns need to be authorized to work in the country where their internship is located. Sponsorship may be available.”
