## Process, as the company describes it

- QRT reviews applications on a rolling basis, so “applying early is recommended”.
- Selected candidates “will complete a coding challenge focused on core programming and problem-solving skills”. For the FPGA and Security roles this step is a technical interview with the team instead.
- Shortlisted candidates then have interviews “either on-site at an assessment centre or via Microsoft Teams”.
- For quant research, QRT encourages candidates to take part in its Data Challenges: “Strong performance may lead to direct follow-up from our team”.
- Strong performance during the internship “may lead to consideration for a full-time graduate role”.
