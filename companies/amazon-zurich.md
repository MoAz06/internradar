## Process, as the company describes it

- Both Zurich roles are with RIVR Technologies AG and are posted on amazon.jobs.
- Applied Science interns take an online test with “a coding assessment and a work styles assessment”; the coding part takes about 70 minutes.
- Applicants are reviewed on a rolling basis and “matched to teams aligned with their experience plus location and availability preferences prior to interviews.”
- In the interview loop “You’ll likely meet with two to seven people”, for 45 minutes to an hour each, and “You can expect to hear from us within five business days.”
- “You can apply and interview for a maximum of 10 roles at a time.”
