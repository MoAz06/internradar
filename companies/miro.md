## Process, as the company describes it

- Miro says it usually replies “within a few business days” after you apply.
- Recruiter screen about your skills, resilience, experience and what you are looking for.
- Skills assessment if the role needs it: “a technical assessment or provide a case study”.
- Hiring manager interview, which “usually takes 90-120 minutes”.
- Meet the Team with cross-functional stakeholders, then a Leadership Round with a regional or organizational leader.
- Decision “within two weeks”. The process has 5-6 rounds and most interviews are on Google Meet.
- This is Miro's general hiring page; it does not say whether graduates follow every step.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. Miro's hiring page says not to be “put off from trying again” if you do not succeed the first time.
