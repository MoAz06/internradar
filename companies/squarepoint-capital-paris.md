## Process, as the company describes it

- Squarepoint asks you to apply to one job only: “Please only apply to the one job you feel best fits your skillset and experience.” If the team thinks another role fits better, it reaches out.
- The application form asks for your GPA (“Grade Point Average (GPA) or equivalent obtained”) and for any other pending offers and their deadlines.
- The form warns that “interviews conducted during our recruitment process may be subject to audio or video recording”. The number of interview rounds is not described.
