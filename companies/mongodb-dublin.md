## Process, as the company describes it

- MongoDB's students page lists the steps: you apply, then a 1st round chat with a recruiter about your experience and motivations.
- The 2nd round is an interview with a current team member.
- The final round is a panel of “2-3 back to back interviews with a manager and other team members”, then an evaluation and the offer.
- No online test is named.
- The postings say it “may take up to six weeks to hear back” on your application.
