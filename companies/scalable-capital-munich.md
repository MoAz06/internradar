## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply online with a CV; “a cover letter is not required”. The AI/ML internship posting also asks for your latest transcript of records.
- Interns and working students first have an initial interview with a team member or the future manager, where “depending on the position, technical topics will also be covered”. Expect questions about the business model.
- Depending on the position, there may be a test assignment.
- The final interview is “(30-60 Min.)” with your future manager or someone from the team.
- For most Engineering positions there is a timed Codility coding challenge of 60 minutes, to be done within 7 days. Below a score of 80% the process stops.
- The whole process usually takes 3 to 4 weeks, and every candidate gets feedback within 5 weekdays.

## Re-apply rule

Scalable says you can reapply for future openings. For a position you were already rejected for, it would “generally recommend to wait at least 6 months before applying again”.
