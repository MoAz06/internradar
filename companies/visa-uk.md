## Process, as the company describes it

- Visa's UK university recruitment page lists six stages: a one-page application form with your CV or LinkedIn profile, online testing, a phone interview, a technical challenge, a final stage, then the offer.
- The online test is “a series of online games that show us how you make decisions”.
- Technology applicants are asked to complete a technical challenge that gives Visa “a better idea of your level of knowledge”.
- The final stage is “an assessment centre or final face-to-face interview in one of our offices”, depending on the role.
- Visa aims to have all graduate and intern roles “advertised on our website by the end of September each year”. The Reading posting went up on 12 November 2026.
