## Process, as the company describes it

- Data Business Analyst internship: if chosen, a member of the Campus Team calls you to check the fit.
- If that goes well, an interview with your future manager, on site or through Teams.
- Mobile Data Analyst stage: after the Campus call you get a practical case to test your technical skills.
- Then an interview (in person or video) with your future internship tutor and the Technical Director.
