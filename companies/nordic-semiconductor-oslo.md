## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply through Nordic's recruitment system; if asked, upload your CV, a short motivational letter and educational transcripts.
- Screening can take a while. Your first contact “might be an invitation to complete one or several digital assignments”.
- “All applicants will hear from us after the application deadline.”
- First interview, physical or digital, normally with your potential new manager.
- If that goes well you may get assessments “such as a technical assignment, skill test, ability test or personality test”, and the second interview can be more technical.
- Some countries add a reference check and a background check before the job offer.
- Placements for students (6 to 12 months, paid, full-time) “are usually published in Autumn”.
