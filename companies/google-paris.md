## Process, as the company describes it

- Postings ask for an updated CV and a transcript in English.
- Google says the hiring process typically takes “roughly 6-8 weeks”.
- You may be asked to take the Google Hiring Assessment, and role-related assessments such as a coding exercise.
- Usually one or two shorter recruiter conversations by phone or video.
- Then a panel of interviews, by video or in person, with no brainteasers.
- You can apply to up to three jobs within a rolling 30 day window.
- The Student Researcher posting says hearing back “can take upwards of 90+ days”.

## Re-apply rule

Google typically asks people to wait at least a year before reapplying for the same type of role. You can apply to at most three jobs in a rolling 30-day window.
