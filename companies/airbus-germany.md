## Process, as the company describes it

- For internships in Germany you “can apply year-round”, and Airbus advises: “Apply online 3-6 months ahead.”
- Postings ask you to upload a cover letter, CV, relevant transcripts and an enrollment certificate.
- After your application is assessed, there is a video or on-site interview with your future manager, and “You'll receive an answer shortly after.”
- The global FAQ says “Some roles may include technical tests, case studies or online assessments.”
- You may apply to more than one role at the same time.
