## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply through the job ad with a CV and motivation letter, a copy of your degree certificate and, for non-EU/EFTA citizens, a university certificate that the internship is mandatory.
- Recruiters rate your CV against the posting, and the hiring manager decides who gets an interview.
- The first phase is “a telephone interview or personal interview (or video interview)”, and further interviews follow after a positive first impression.
- You get feedback from the recruiter after each personal interview, and Roche says rejected applicants “will definitely be informed”.
- Roche says a recruitment process takes “1.5-3 months”. The Basel posting went up on 24 November 2026.
