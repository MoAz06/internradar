## Process, as the company describes it

- Internships run all year: “Staże dostępne są przez cały rok, trwają przez co najmniej 12 tygodni” (all year, at least 12 weeks).
- You apply online, on NVIDIA's site or with your LinkedIn profile; NVIDIA recommends you “limit your applications to the top three to five roles that best match your interests and experience.”
- The How We Hire FAQ says “Interns will typically complete phone interviews only.”
- Technical roles may include a coding exercise, “usually done using Hackerrank”.
- Using tools such as ChatGPT in an interview without approval “will result in the disqualification of candidacy.”
- Selected candidates should hear back “within a couple of weeks of their application.”
