## Process, as the company describes it

- All PA early careers roles follow the same process. It starts with an application form about your studies and motivation, then task-based online assessments that are not timed, though some tasks “require you to respond quickly by tapping on your keyboard”.
- The second stage depends on the role: a review of your application by the business, a technical assessment, or a presentation on a set topic.
- The final stage is an assessment event and a final interview, virtual or face to face depending on the role. It can include one-to-one interviews, group exercises, presentations and case studies, and some roles add coding assessments and technical interviews.
- The three postings list an assessment centre with technical assessments and final stage interviews.
- PA aims to confirm next steps within two to three weeks of each stage, and asks that no AI tools, including AI note takers, are used in interviews.

## Re-apply rule

You can apply for only one early careers role. If you are rejected at any stage “you will need to wait six months before applying for another role”.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: “Salary: £27,000” on the London Digital Trust & Cyber Security summer internship posting.
- Pay: “Salary potential: £36,500” on the London Digital & Data graduate posting.
- Pay: “Salary potential: £31,000 with additional £3,500 transport allowance” on the Cambridge Electronic Engineer graduate posting.
- Visa: “PA are unable to provide sponsorship for those who do not have the right to work in the UK.”
- All candidates must get security clearance. PA says time abroad, such as living outside the UK for more than six months in the last five years, can affect it.
- Office: London roles are hybrid with the London Victoria office as base. The Electronic Engineer role is based at the Global Innovation & Technology Centre in Melbourn, near Cambridge.
