## Process, as the company describes it

- ADI says its interviews “can be on the phone, via video conference, or in person”.
- For technical interviews ADI advises: “Be prepared to show your work.”
- ADI asks you to “Be specific about projects” you have “worked on or led”.
- For placement students, ADI says “we view your time on placement student as an extended interview” and may “consider you for full-time opportunities after graduation”.
- For positions with access to technical data, applicants other than US citizens, US permanent residents and protected individuals “may have to go through an export licensing review process”.
