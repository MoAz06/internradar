## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online, can link your LinkedIn profile, and pick your preferred Shell area.
- Within 24 hours you get an invitation to the Virtual Job Tryout, which you finish within seven days; with the On-Demand Interview it takes “approximately 40 minutes.”
- Next is “a virtual case study session with two Shell employees”; graduate applicants also have a virtual interview with the same two.
- Shell looks at Capacity, Achievement and Relationships, “which we call the CAR criteria”.
- Interns are matched to a stream (Finance, Information & Digital Technology, Trading & Supply or Integrated Commercial), and “Success in the internship could lead to an offer for the Shell Graduate Programme.”

## Re-apply rule

“Yes, you may re-apply to the programme after 10 months.” A new application within 10 months of a rejection is “automatically regretted”.

## Pay, relocation, visa
