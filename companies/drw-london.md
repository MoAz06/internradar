## Process, as the company describes it

- DRW reviews applications “on a rolling basis” and only asks for your most recent resume.
- Next is a technical challenge you do at home under timed conditions: a quantitative challenge on probability and logic, or a coding challenge, depending on the role.
- Then a Zoom interview with a developer, quantitative trader or researcher, with technical and behavioral questions.
- The final round runs over two days: a technical challenge in the afternoon of day one, then a full day of technical and behavioral interviews.
- DRW says your process may be “slightly different”, as it is agile in everything it does.
