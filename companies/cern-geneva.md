## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on SmartRecruiters with a CV in English or French. The two summer programmes also need an academic reference letter “dated less than 12 months”, an official transcript and a certificate of enrolment for the whole summer. The Short Term Internship needs only a CV.
- CERN says your application “will be reviewed and you may be invited to take part in an online interview with the hiring manager(s)”. If you are successful you get an offer.
- Recruiters and hiring managers review applications together. CERN's general page says later stages may include “asynchronous video interviews, technical assessments, panel discussions, or informal chats”, depending on the team.
- Both summer programmes close “21/01/27 at 23:59 (Geneva time)”. The openlab pages say students “will get a reply to their application by early April 2027”. The main summer programme gives no reply date.
- The Short Term Internship postings close “01/11/2027 at 23:59 (Geneva time)” and the start date is “to be discussed”. openlab students cannot pick their project, they are assigned one based on their application. CERN says every candidate gets a response, but “personalized feedback isn’t feasible”.

## Re-apply rule

CERN's tips page says “You’re welcome to reapply.” For the summer programmes you are not eligible if you already took part in the Summer Student Programme or worked at CERN for more than 3 months under another status.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: “94 CHF per calendar day (tax free)” subsistence allowance on the Geneva Summer Student and openlab Summer Student postings.
- Pay: “A monthly allowance of 1,593 Swiss Francs.” on the Geneva Short Term Internship postings. Paid leave only for contracts of 4 months or more.
- Insurance (summer programmes): CERN's health insurance, “contribution already deducted from allowance”. Students paid by a national funding agency must bring their own insurance.
- Travel (summer programmes): “Travel expenses to Geneva upon joining CERN, depending on your individual circumstances.” The openlab FAQ says a lump-sum travel allowance is paid at the end of your stay.
- Housing: help to find accommodation on the CERN site or nearby for summer students. The Short Term Internship postings and page list no travel, insurance or housing help.
- Office: all five postings are “Fully Onsite”, 40 hours a week. openlab summer students cannot telework and get no holidays.

## Notes

- Nationality, summer programmes: “Candidates of all nationalities are welcome to apply”. Nationals of Canada, Japan or the United States must also apply through their national websites.
- Nationality, Short Term Internship: only nationals of a CERN Member or Associate Member State. CERN currently cannot consider Pakistani, Lithuanian, Latvian, Cypriot, Croatian and Turkish nationals because a membership ceiling has been reached.
- Summer programmes: Bachelor's or Master's students (not PhD) with at least 6 semesters of full-time study by the start of the summer, still enrolled full time during the stay, with good English. openlab asks for “a strong computing profile”.
- Duration: the main summer programme runs 8 to 13 weeks (the posting says 2 months, ideal start 14/6/27). openlab runs nine weeks, starting 21 June or 28 June 2027.
- Short Term Internship: 1 to 6 months, for diploma, Bachelor's or Master's students who are “required or encouraged by your university to complete a practical training period”, aged at least 18, with good English or French. There are postings per field, including IT, Data Science, AI & Analytics and Electronics Engineering.
- Several applications: “If multiple positions catch your interest, you can apply to each one.” Each one is reviewed on its own. No rule is stated on applying to both summer programmes in the same year.
- Technical Studentship (monthly net allowance 3486 CHF, 4 to 12 months) and Doctoral Studentship (3907 CHF) had no open positions on 25 November 2026.
