## Process, as the company describes it

- Swisscom asks only for “your CV or your LinkedIn profile”; a cover letter is not required.
- Depending on the role there are up to three ways to apply, including a “Tech Challenge” that takes 20 to 90 minutes.
- Swisscom says it gets back to you “within 15 working days”.
- If your application fits, there is an initial interview in person, by phone or by video; later interviews are preferably face to face.
- Other formats used for some roles: a group interview (“Speed Dating”) with agile teams, an assessment of a few hours up to a full day near the end, and an experience day shadowing a team.
- Swisscom does not accept speculative applications; you apply only to advertised vacancies.

## Re-apply rule

After a rejection you may apply for other jobs: the FAQ answers “Of course!” No waiting period is stated.
