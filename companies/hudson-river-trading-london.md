## Process, as the company describes it

- HRT's student page says technical interviews “usually consist of a coding test, 1-2 technical phone screens, and an onsite” multi-round final interview. Length and topics vary by role.
- Software engineering applicants pick C++ or Python for their interviews when they apply.
- You may only apply once: “Please apply to the ONE role you are most interested in”. HRT then considers you for all open positions.
- Application review can take up to 2 weeks.
- AI tools are banned in interviews and assessments unless HRT says otherwise.
