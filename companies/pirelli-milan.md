## Process, as the company describes it

- In a 2017 article Pirelli wrote that interns are chosen “After an online application, and two structured interviews, one on the phone and the other one as a video interview”, followed by an assessment.
- Pirelli said then that “Assessments take place every Wednesday in the historical Room 31 at Piero Pirelli Institute.”
- Pirelli also wrote that “one intern out of three is hired by the department where they completed their internship.” New graduates join a two-year programme called WarmingUp@Pirelli.
