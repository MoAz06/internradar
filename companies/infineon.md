## Process, as the company describes it

- Students (interns, working students, thesis): apply online, you get a confirmation email, then “an initial interview will take place” with the responsible department.
- “Depending on the position, a second interview may take place.” Then a contract offer.
- Internship postings ask for a CV in English, enrolment certificate, the study regulations for the mandatory internship (if applicable) and a recent transcript.
- IGP: CV screening, an interview call with your potential department manager, a recruiting day or extended interview with group or individual assessment sessions, then an offer.
- No application deadlines; “Once a position is no longer visible in our job search, it means the position has already been filled.” Unsolicited applications are not accepted.
