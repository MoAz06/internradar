## Process, as the company describes it

- Software Developer Graduate: the posting lists “Apply > Technical Screen > Recruiter Interview > Technical Interview > Final Interview > Offer”.
- Site Reliability Engineer Graduate: “Apply → Technical Screen → Recruiter Interview → Technical Interview(s) → Final Interview → Offer”.
- Site Reliability Co-Op placement: “Apply > Technical Assessment > Recruiter Interview > Technical Interview > Offer”.
- Quantitative Strategy Developer internship: “a series of interviews including coding, mathematics, system design and behavioural interviews”.
- Most of these roles are hired “on a rolling basis”, so SIG says to apply early.
- The quantitative research and systematic trading postings do not describe the interview steps.
