## Process, as the company describes it

- Helsing's FAQ lists the steps as application review, a screening call with a Talent Partner, a 1st stage interview, “~2-3 interviews (depending on the role)”, a final interview with senior leadership, then the offer.
- The engineering handbook says the 1st Stage is a “high-pass filter” and that “after passing the 1st Stage, there will be around 3 technical interviews”, followed by a final with leadership.
- Interviews are virtual by default, and onsite is possible on request. The handbook says the whole process “can take upwards of 6-weeks”.
- Early Careers - Electronics and RF Engineering uses a separate optional route (BSA2A): instead of a CV and recruiter screen, you fill in a short form, then upload a video walkthrough of up to 10 minutes of something you built.
- After the video come two 60-minute technical interviews (a “BSA Deep Dive” on your project, then a requirements and design interview) and a 30-minute final with engineering leadership.
- The PhD research internship and the Formula Student Programme postings do not describe their own interview steps.

## Re-apply rule

Helsing asks you to wait at least 6 months after a rejection before applying again.
