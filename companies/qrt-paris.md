## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online. QRT says “We review applications on a rolling basis, so applying early is recommended”.
- For the software, data, infrastructure and research roles, selected candidates “will complete a coding challenge focused on core programming and problem-solving skills”.
- For the security role the assessment step is “a technical interview with the relevant team” instead.
- Shortlisted candidates then interview “either on-site at an assessment centre or via Microsoft Teams”.
- Research applicants are encouraged to take part in one of QRT's Data Challenges; “Strong performance may lead to direct follow-up from our team”.
