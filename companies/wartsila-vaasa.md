## Process, as the company describes it

- Summer Power 2027 has one job post for all white-collar (office) trainee positions and one for blue-collar positions, so “you can apply to all open white-collar or blue-collar Summer Trainee positions with just one application”.
- Applications only through the recruitment system: “We do not approve applications nor CVs via e-mail.”
- Hiring managers get access to candidates in the second week of January and start screening; “Candidates matching the need of the team will be invited to a job interview.”
- Applications are processed as they arrive and managers hire as soon as they find a match, so Wärtsilä recommends applying early.
- The goal is that “all hiring decisions would be made by the end of March”, and applicants should hear by email by 31.3.2027.
- If you are interviewed but not chosen, your application stays active so other managers can contact you.

## Re-apply rule

Applications from last year are not valid: “The old applications are no longer valid for this recruitment.” You have to apply again.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Relocation: “no relocation costs nor immigration support are available for trainee positions”.
- Visa: you must arrange any work permit and residence yourself, at your own cost.
