## Process, as the company describes it

- Step 1: an online application with your CV. UBS does not ask for cover letters “in most regions”.
- Step 2: online assessments (verbal, numerical, inductive-logical reasoning and a “UBS Culture Match”). Which ones you get depends on the business area. They “need to be completed within seven days of receiving the invitation email”, and you cannot retake them.
- You skip the assessments if you have “already taken and passed the online assessments in the last six months”.
- Step 3: if you pass, “you might receive a link to record your video interview”.
- Step 4: “The last stage of the process is our final round virtual or in-person interviews.”
- Rejected candidates do not get detailed feedback.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. For Switzerland you may send at most three applications in total across all UBS divisions and Swiss offices in the same academic year. Assessments cannot be retaken, but a pass from the last six months is reused.
