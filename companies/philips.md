## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on the careers site and get an automatic confirmation email.
- A member of the Talent Acquisition team tells you whether you are shortlisted.
- Your first conversation is with Talent Acquisition about the role, the team and Philips. If it does not work out, Philips says “we'll let you know precisely why”.
- Next comes an interview that “will likely be with the hiring manager or a member of the team”, in person or by video. There may be several interviews.
- After a decision you get a final offer and agree on a start date.
- Philips says each recruitment journey “differs a little” by role, team and location. This is the general NL page, not a student-specific one.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. Philips's FAQ says it would be happy to consider you again and asks you to submit a separate application for each role you want.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: monthly full-time allowance of €500 to €700 gross, depending on education level; a different allowance if you relocate to the Netherlands for the internship.
- Housing: €300 net a month near Amsterdam, €255 net at other Philips sites, if your home is more than 50 km or 1.5 hours away.
- Travel: up to €192 net if you have no free student public transport.
- Visa: not mentioned.
