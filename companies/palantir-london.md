## Process, as the company describes it

- Palantir says the number and format of interviews depend on your skills and the role.
- “Everyone starts with one or two phone interviews”; if there is a fit, you are brought onsite for a round of in-person interviews (the on-site can also be virtual).
- The process differs between Software Engineer and Forward Deployed Software Engineer roles.
- Team placement for interns and new grads is decided using what Palantir learns in the interviews plus business needs.
- Forward Deployed postings say that by applying you commit to deciding “within two weeks of receiving your written offer”.
