## Process, as the company describes it

- For student and graduate SDE roles, the online assessment (OA) “is the first step in the hiring process”.
- The SDE internship OA has a coding assessment, a workstyles assessment and a feedback survey, and “takes about 90 minutes to complete”.
- The full-time SDE OA adds a work simulation of about 60 minutes on top of the coding and workstyles parts.
- After the OA, “If you are moved forward in the process for an interview, Amazon will reach out directly.”
- For the Applied Scientist PhD internship you submit one application and are matched with science teams; the recruiter confirms the team's start date and duration preferences before interviewing.
