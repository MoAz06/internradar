## Process, as the company describes it

- GetYourGuide lists “six core application stages” on its how-we-hire page, and says the process “may look slightly different for some roles”.
- Application review, then a “Call with recruiter”.
- A take-home assignment: “a short task to evaluate your coding skills and developer knowledge”. The page shows two orders, with the take-home either before or after the technical interview.
- A technical interview on “your technical knowledge and problem-solving skills”.
- “Meet your team”: you talk with “an Engineering Manager and other future colleagues”, then a final offer.
- In technical evaluations “you’ll be told if using AI is expected and welcomed”.
- Feedback calls are only for candidates “who've reached the face-to-face stage”.
- The page is for all roles. The working student posting itself only says to submit a CV in English.
