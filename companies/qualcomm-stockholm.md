## Process, as the company describes it

- All openings are on careers.qualcomm.com, and “The same application process is used for positions in all locations.”
- You may apply to as many roles as you like; if you match, “a member of our talent team will contact you to schedule an interview.”
- For Sweden: “August – September: positions are opened for applicants” and “March – April: positions are closed”.
- The EMEA page gives no length for Sweden; the Stockholm posting is 6 months.
- You must be enrolled in a Bachelor's, Master's or PhD “with at least one semester or term remaining following the internship.”
- Qualcomm uses an AI tool from Eightfold to score how well you match a role, and you agree not to use AI tools in interviews.
