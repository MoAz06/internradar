## Process, as the company describes it

- Da Vinci's FAQ says the process “includes three steps: CV submission, assessments, and interviews.”
- Applications go through the careers page only; email or LinkedIn applications are not processed.
- The assessment depends on the role: quant researchers do “a numerical quiz as well as a programming challenge”, software engineers do a coding challenge.
- For the Quant Trading Intern the posting lists: application, a one minute video introduction, an AI trading challenge, an online technical discussion, onsite final assessments, offer.
- Da Vinci asks you to apply for one position at a time and list other roles of interest in your motivation letter.

## Re-apply rule

Candidates may take the same assessment only once. If you fail it, you “will not be able to re-apply for any role that entails that same assessment.”

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Graduate Quant: “Sign-on bonus of EUR 20k, base salary of EUR 100k”, plus variable pay.
- Relocation (Graduate Quant): relocation budget, flights, house-finding service and expat support when moving from abroad.
- Quant Trading Intern: “Competitive compensation”, no figure; flights and stay in Amsterdam are covered.
- Visa: both application forms ask if you need visa sponsorship for the Netherlands; no policy is stated.
