## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply through the form on GSA's site. Round 1 is two remote technical interviews “via HackerRank or Zoom”, each up to one hour.
- Then comes a take-home exercise done in your own time, and during it a call with the recruiting team.
- Round 2 is up to about four hours of interviews, onsite or remote if needed, with technical and team-specific questions.
- Final rounds are talks with senior or technical leadership. GSA says this path is for core research and technology roles and “can flex a little”.
- GSA says its process “isn't the shortest” and gives more specific feedback to candidates who reach the later stages.
