## Process, as the company describes it

- Internships: “Binnen 5 werkdagen reactie” (an answer within 5 working days), then a first meeting. You and your future manager agree on the assignment, then sign the internship agreement.
- IT Management Traineeship: motivation questions by email, an intelligence and learning test, a first interview, a business case, a second interview, then the contract.
- Other jobs: an interview, an assessment and a second interview, then pre-employment screening.
