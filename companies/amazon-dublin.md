## Process, as the company describes it

- Amazon's student SDE page lists four steps: apply, online assessment, virtual interviews, offer.
- If the recruiting team wants to move forward, “you'll be asked to do an Online Assessment (OA)”.
- Depending on the OA, you may be invited to interview; interviewers “will ask about past experiences and specific situations”.
- After the interview you hear your result “within five business days”.
- You can apply to many roles, but “you'll only be considered for one role at a time.”
- This process is from the SDE page; the network, system engineer and cloud support postings do not describe their own steps.
