## Process, as the company describes it

- For the technology internship, Marshall Wace aims “to respond to everyone within 10 days of their application or previous round”.
- Interviews run “on a rolling basis”.
- The final stage is in person: “We will not complete the final stage virtually.”
- Feedback is aimed “within one working week of your interview or test”.
- Apply to one role and one location only; duplicate applications delay processing.
- The aim of the internship is “to convert the best performers into graduates”.
