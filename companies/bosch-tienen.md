## Process, as the company describes it

- You fill in the online form on the Bosch job board with your CV and cover letter, at most 10 MB.
- A confirmation mail comes within minutes; depending on the role you hear back “binnen de twee à drie weken”.
- If your application convinces them, Bosch invites you for an interview and possibly a tour of the company.
- The Tienen plant takes 10 to 15 interns all year round from several countries; most live in Tienen or Leuven.
