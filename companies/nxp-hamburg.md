## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online with your CV and get a confirmation email. You can apply to more than one role at a time with the same candidate profile.
- German postings ask for “a current transcript of records”; master's students send their bachelor's transcript too. Applications with them “will be given priority”.
- NXP's general steps: a phone interview with a recruiter first, then one or more virtual or in-person interviews, now and then with a panel.
- NXP aims to get back to you within two weeks of an interview. It doesn't give interview feedback.
- Internships in Germany are mandatory internships only: “Aufgrund von internen Richtlinien können wir nur Pflichtpraktika anbieten.”
- Working students work 8 to 20 hours a week for 6 months or longer; the Hamburg and Munich postings say up to 18 hours.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. NXP's FAQ: “If you were not selected for a particular role, you may always reapply to a similar or alternate role at any time.”

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: working students in Hamburg and Munich get €19.40 an hour, in Hamburg €20.00 from the 7th month.
- A Hamburg internship posting gives “2,120.00€” without naming the period.
- Housing and travel: NXP doesn't provide housing, relocation or transport during the internship. “If you have to relocate, there might be a small support (depending on the job location).”
- Visa: “Work visa sponsorship depends on the position requirements and each individual's qualifications.”
