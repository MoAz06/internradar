## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on Sierra's Ashby board with your resume.
- Sierra's early career page lists four steps: apply, a technical assessment (“Demonstrate how you think and solve problems”), “Meet the team”, where you meet future teammates and “collaborate on real-world scenarios”, and a decision with “Fast feedback and next steps”.
- The number of interviews inside “Meet the team” and the platform for the assessment are not stated.
- Sierra recommends applying to the one role you are most excited about. Recruiters will tell you if another role fits you better.
- Timing is not fixed: “Timelines can vary, especially during peak application periods”.
