## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online with your CV and get a confirmation email. “Applying only takes a minute!”
- Dutch postings: “After a positive screening based on your CV you will have an initial video conversation with our Talent Acquisition Consultant followed by several business interviews.”
- NXP's general steps: a phone interview with a recruiter first, then one or more virtual or in-person interviews, now and then with a panel.
- NXP aims to get back to you within two weeks of an interview. It doesn't give interview feedback.
- You can apply to more than one role at a time with the same candidate profile.
- Interns must be “registered as a student during the entire period”. Internships are full time (about 40 hours a week) and usually last 3 to 12 months; a thesis takes about 6 months.

## Re-apply rule

No waiting period is stated. NXP's FAQ: “If you were not selected for a particular role, you may always reapply to a similar or alternate role at any time.”

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: no figure given in the Dutch postings.
- Housing and travel: NXP doesn't provide housing, relocation or transport during the internship. “If you have to relocate, there might be a small support (depending on the job location).”
- Visa: “Work visa sponsorship depends on the position requirements and each individual's qualifications.”
