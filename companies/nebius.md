## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply with your resume and “hear back from us within a maximum of three business days”.
- Next is a call with a talent acquisition specialist about the role and your background.
- Nebius's backend interview page: a preliminary interview with “one task covering coding/algorithms and data structures”, coded in an online editor.
- Backend page: for junior and middle levels the technical interviews are “two sections with tasks covering coding/algorithms and data structures”. “Each interview lasts one hour.”
- Nebius's SRE interview page: “three technical interviews”, each “between 60 to 90 minutes”, then “a final hour-long interview”.
- Within a week after each interview, your recruiter contacts you about the next steps.
- Both Early Talent postings offer the chance to be considered for a full-time role after the programme.
