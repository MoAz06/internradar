## Pay, relocation, visa

- Paid: “the full-time paid internship lasts six months”, no figure. The postings mention “a fair market salary”.
- Relocation: none. “For residents outside of Switzerland, you will have to find your own accommodation in order to apply for the L or B work permit.”
