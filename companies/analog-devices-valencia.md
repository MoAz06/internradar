## Process, as the company describes it

- ADI says its interviews “can be on the phone, via video conference, or in person”. For technical interviews: “Be prepared to show your work.”
- “At ADI we view your time on placement student as an extended interview”, and interns are the first to be considered for full-time roles.
- For positions with access to technical data, applicants other than US citizens, US permanent residents and protected individuals “may have to go through an export licensing review process”.
- Valencia: “We will be recruiting for Co-op positions from after September 2026.” ADI prefers long internships of more than 6 months; the main intern posting is for students in the last year of a BSc or master's.
