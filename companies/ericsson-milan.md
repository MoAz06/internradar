## Process, as the company describes it

- Ericsson's general process: after you apply, you typically have one or two shorter conversations over the phone or video, usually with a recruiter.
- “Depending on the role, you will have between one to three interviews.” A case interview or assessment is used for certain positions only.
- Then a reference check, an offer and a contract.
- Italian internships are extracurricular internships (Tirocinio Extracurriculare) in the office five days a week, in Milan or Rome.
- Early Career Program: an online assessment, a recruiter call and 2 to 3 interviews, sometimes with a case. You do the assessment once, even if you apply to several roles.
- You can apply to more than one role. At the end you get an email with the final outcome.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: engineering intern postings in Milan and Rome give “a monthly internship reimbursement of €1.000 and meal vouchers valued at €9.”
- Graduates: the Early Career Program posting in Rome gives a starting salary of EUR 43,200, under the national collective agreement for telecommunications.
- Housing and visa: not stated for Italy. For the Early Career Program “Ericsson supports work permits”, if you are based in or ready to move to the role's location.
