## Process, as the company describes it

- Microsoft's student hiring page says “You'll have an initial interview and, if selected, a second final interview.”
- “All our interviews are virtual.”
- For some openings “you may be asked to write code, share a creative portfolio or project, or provide examples of your work in other ways”.
- At the first round screen you are assessed for a functional role, “like mechanical engineer or software engineer”, and considered for all teams that hire for it. At the final round you are assessed “for a specific role within an organization”.
- If you are picked for interviews, a recruiter or scheduler contacts you “within 90 days of their application submission”. Microsoft asks you to “allow up to 90 days to receive a decision on your application”.

## Re-apply rule

Interviewing last year does not let you skip a round: “All candidates must submit a new application each year.”
