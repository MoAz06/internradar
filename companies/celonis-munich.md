## Process, as the company describes it

- The Galaxy page lists four steps. First a recruiter “video call with one of our recruiters to learn more about the program”.
- Second, an “Interview with a manager of the Galaxy Program to understand your motivation and interest in process mining.”
- Third, a challenge: “Complete a challenge exercise and interview with dedicated hiring managers, team leads, and/or team members.”
- Last, a “Final interview with a member of the senior management team.”
- The posting says Celonis uses “an artificial intelligence tool to match the information from your resume” to its job descriptions; final decisions are made by people.
- The FAQ says candidates who “speak to individuals at Celonis and read about us online tend to perform best” in interviews.
