## Process, as the company describes it

- For SDE roles the online assessment “is the first step in the hiring process for our full-time and internship SDE roles”.
- For SDE internships the OA has a coding assessment, a workstyles assessment and a feedback survey, and “takes about 90 minutes to complete”.
- For full-time SDE roles the OA adds a work simulation; you should “set aside up to two hours of uninterrupted time for the OA”.
- After the OA: “If you are moved forward in the process for an interview, Amazon will reach out directly.”
- For the Applied Scientist PhD internship the OA “consists of a coding assessment and a work styles assessment”, and one application is matched with science teams.
