## Process, as the company describes it

- Graduate postings: after you apply you get an email confirmation, and “The hiring team will review your application together with the hiring manager.”
- “Shortlisted candidates will be contacted with information about the following steps.”
- The general Volvo Group process: a first interview “in person, via video conference, or on the phone”, then possibly “second interviews, assessments and references”.
- Interviews usually cover “technical, behavioral, and motivational aspects”.
- The graduate postings say “a background check as well as alcohol and drug testing may be conducted”.
- The Data Analyst posting asks for a cover letter on how you are building leadership skills and on one data analysis experience.
