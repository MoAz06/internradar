## Process, as the company describes it

- Datadog says its process “differs slightly depending on the roles, teams, and departments”, and that one of the steps is expected to happen in person.
- Initial screen: “in most cases” by phone or Zoom with the recruiter and or hiring manager.
- Face-to-face interview, which “may consist of multiple rounds or panels”.
- Depending on the role, a take-home project.
- After all interviews the team meets to pick a candidate; if selected, the recruiter contacts you with an offer.
- Postings ask you to submit your CV in English.
- You can apply to several positions at once.
