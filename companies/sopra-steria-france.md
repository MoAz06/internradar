## Process, as the company describes it

- Sopra Steria France's careers site describes a five-step process for all candidates: you apply, they review your application, then a phone pre-interview with HR.
- At that phone step, depending on the job, “un recruteur vous proposera de participer à un test” to show your skills.
- Next come one or more interviews, on site or remote, with HR or the operational staff who will manage you.
- The recruiter then calls you back with the answer, positive or negative. The whole process takes “en moyenne 20 jours”.
- The page is not specific to internships. The students site etudiants.soprasteria.fr did not load, so its process page was not read.

## Notes

- Most French internships are in the Toulouse area (Colomiers, Toulouse), Île-de-France (Courbevoie, Le Plessis-Robinson, Paris), Aix-en-Provence and Nantes.
- Most tech postings are a 6-month “stage de fin d’études” for final-year engineering school or Master 2 computer science students.
- Postings are in French. The Biot and Courbevoie postings ask for professional English.
- Topics include Java and Angular development (Nantes), SysOps with Linux, Ansible and Kubernetes (Biot) and endpoint security, EPP and EDR (Courbevoie).
- The postings say many permanent (CDI) jobs can follow the internship.
