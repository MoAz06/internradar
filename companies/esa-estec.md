## Process, as the company describes it

- Internships go online on jobs.esa.int every November. You can apply to two at most: “Should you apply to more vacancies in this call, all your applications will be disregarded.”
- You make a candidate profile with your CV and a motivation letter.
- Shortlisting and selection run from December to February. In the interview “your motivation for applying to this role will be explored”, and ESA may ask for selection tests.
- “All candidates will be informed of the outcome of their application.”
- Graduate Trainee positions go online every February. You can apply to three at most, and the interviews are on Microsoft Teams.

## Re-apply rule

Not stated. In one call you can apply to two internships at most; apply to more and all of them are disregarded.
