## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply online; clicking Apply asks you to create an account.
- If your profile matches, you meet a Talent Acquisition Partner for a values and competency interview.
- Then a manager interview that goes into your technical and soft skills; there is no fixed interview order and there may be extra interviews.
- Depending on the role, an English test, a cognitive test and/or a coding assessment.
- The FAQ says a coding test might be required for developers or technical roles.
- You can apply to several positions at the same time.
- Dassault Systèmes says it does not accept unsolicited applications.
