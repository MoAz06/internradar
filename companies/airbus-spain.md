## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online on the Airbus Workday board. The Spanish programmes are tagged #Discover (graduates) and #Xplorer (final-year students).
- The Discover I 2027-2028 postings say “The selection process will be during October 2026 until January 2027.”
- Airbus Spain says: “After your application assessment, you get to demonstrate your motivation during a video interview.” It adds: “You will get an answer shortly after this interview.”
- For Xplorer, Airbus says it publishes vacancies in April and recruits “between April and January”, and that “After assessment, the recruitment process takes a few months.” The postings send questions about the selection to an address at Randstad.
- The global Airbus FAQ says “Some roles may include technical tests, case studies or online assessments”, and that you may apply for more than one role at the same time. The Spanish pages do not name a test for these programmes.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: no figure on the Getafe postings. The Discover postings promise “an attractive study grant” and the Network and Storage posting a “Scholarship Opportunity”.
- Xplorer: the general #XPLORER 2027 INTERNSHIP PROGRAMME posting gives “Grant: 450 EUR gross per month.” for 25 hours a week. The Xplorer AI deployment posting gives no figure.
- Discover includes a Master with Camilo José Cela University. Airbus Spain lists “a funded masters degree” and transport to and from the sites, depending on the site.
- Hours: Discover is full time, “40h/week”. Xplorer is part time, “5h / day - 25 hours per week (8h-13h)”.

## Notes

- Discover: an 11-month full-time internship combined with a Master in three training blocks, built with Camilo José Cela University. Airbus Spain says it is for people who have finished their degree: “the only requirement is to have finished their degree.” You can apply in your final year “as long as you graduate before the internship start date.”
- Discover I 2027-2028: the postings say “Starting date March 2027 until February 2028” and “more than 70 vacancies”. The Getafe software posting also says the internship starts “in early October 2026”, which conflicts with the March date.
- Discover II: Airbus Spain's pages do not explain the name. The graduates page lists two intakes, “Early March” and “Early October”, but does not say which one is I or II. One Discover II 2026-2027 posting (Service Data Integration, Getafe) is open; it does not give a start date.
- Xplorer: a curricular internship with a Bachelor's final project (TFG) for students in the last year of their degree. Airbus Spain says “Duration: 5-6 months” and “Start Date: February”. The AI deployment posting runs “from February to July 2027” and asks for English B2 and Spanish B1.
- Getafe tech postings: Software Engineering DevOps, Test Automation and AI Agents (Python, C++, Linux, Docker, CI/CD; degree in Computer Science, Telecommunications or Data Science; English B2). Network and Storage Systems (asks for a student “Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree”, unlike the other Discover postings). Xplorer AI deployment to support production (dashboard on Skywise; Python and SQL valued).
- Sevilla: #Discover I 2027-2028 AI Repair Solutions at Sevilla Tablada, asking for a degree in Data Analytics and Visualization and English B2. Workday lists it as part time, but the text says full time, 40 h a week.
- Illescas: Airbus Spain names it as a site. The open Illescas postings for these programmes are in industrial robotics, automation and quality, not software.
