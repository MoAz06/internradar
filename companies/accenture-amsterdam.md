## Process, as the company describes it

- You get an email that your application came in, then a recruiter reviews it.
- If you are selected, interviews by phone, video or in person. How many depends on the role.
- Some roles have an online assessment, usually within 72 hours and 30 to 90 minutes long.
- Then the offer.

## Re-apply rule

You can apply again: “Yes, you're welcome to reapply or look for a role that may be a better fit.” A failed assessment can be retaken after 90 days at the earliest.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Two of the thesis internships: €500 a month for 40 hours, plus travel costs.
- The others say an allowance is paid, without a figure.

## Notes

- Every thesis internship here asks for a master's at a Dutch university.
