## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online on Accenture's Workday board. Accenture's own hiring page says you get an email to confirm, then recruiters review your application. They may also point you to other roles, which need a separate application.
- If you move on, Accenture calls or emails you to set up interviews. They are held by phone, video or in person, and “The number of interviews you’ll have depends on the role.”
- For some roles there is an online test on HackerRank. The page says it is done in one sitting, “typically within 72 hours” of the invite, and most take about 30 to 90 minutes. External AI tools are not allowed, and scoring is automatic: only candidates above the threshold move on.
- If you pass all steps you get an offer. Accenture says applying takes “usually about 15 minutes” and gives no time to a decision: your recruiter gives updates. None of the three Polish intern postings describes its own interview steps.

## Re-apply rule

Yes. Accenture’s hiring page says “you’re welcome to reapply”, and each role needs a new application. If you fail the online test, you must wait at least 90 days before you take the same test again, even for another role.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: no figure on the Warsaw or Łódź postings. All three say “Paid, several-month internship program”.
- Hybrid. The LLM Engineer posting says “Warsaw - Hybrid (2-3 days in the office)”. All three mention flexible hours and the “possibility to work remotely”.

## Notes

- Three open intern roles: AI/MLOps Internship Program (Warsaw, also Kraków), LLM Engineer Internship Program (Warsaw) and Software Testing Internship Program (Łódź, also Katowice).
- At least 3 months, 4 to 5 days a week. The AI/MLOps role asks for “Active student status” and at least 30 hours a week. The testing role asks for at least 32 hours a week and to be based in Łódź or Katowice.
- Polish is required for two roles: the AI/MLOps role asks for English and Polish, the testing role for English B2 and Polish C1. The LLM Engineer posting does not name a language.
- Each posting says there is “a high probability” of a full-time job offer at the end.
