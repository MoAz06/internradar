## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply on Qualcomm's job site: “feel free to apply to as many positions as you'd like!” “The same application process is used for positions in all locations.”
- If your qualifications match, someone from the talent team contacts you to schedule an interview and helps you prepare.
- Interview results are reviewed and the most qualified candidate moves on to the offer, which your recruiter presents.
- Qualcomm's interview policy: “I agree not to use any AI tools, bots, large language models (LLMs), or automated systems to generate responses, complete assessments, or assist during interviews”. Recording or transcribing interviews is not allowed either.
- Interns must be “currently enrolled in a Bachelors, Masters, or PhD seeking program with at least one semester or term remaining following the internship.”
- Ireland: positions open in July and close in March. Internships last 3 to 12 months.
