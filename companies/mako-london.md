## Process, as the company describes it

- Shortly after you apply you get an online aptitude test. Mako says it is scored automatically and is used before anyone reviews your CV.
- If you pass and your profile fits, you take an online technical coding test.
- Next come two rounds of live coding interviews, “conducted in C++”.
- The last step is a final interview with a panel of senior engineers and HR, then offers are made.
- The early careers page describes an older, shorter version (aptitude and problem-solving tests, a first interview on your interest, then a technical round). The posting is the newer one.
