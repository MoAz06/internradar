## Process, as the company describes it

- “the application process generally starts with a résumé screening”.
- Then “certain applicants are invited to take on a few tasks, before moving on to the interviews phase”.
- Postings say: “If you pass our screening, you’ll be asked to complete one or more tests.”
- “We accept only one application per candidate at a time.”
- “It’s rare for us to set an application deadline”.

## Re-apply rule

If you applied and got no offer, Bending Spoons asks you to wait at least one year before applying again for a similar job.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Postings: “Typical annual salary offers range from £85,797 to £151,436 in London and from €66,065 to €107,837 elsewhere in Europe.”
- Interns: “All Spooners, including our interns, get paid competitively.”
- Relocation to Italy, Poland, Spain or the UK: the package covers “all reasonable transportation and temporary accommodation expenses”.
- Visa: “Currently, we can sponsor a visa or residency permit for Italy, Poland, Spain, or the UK”.
- Office: “working on site isn’t required unless explicitly stated”, but in-person time is recommended, “particularly during your first year”.
