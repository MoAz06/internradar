## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply through the online tool. Sensirion says it reads every application and gives “initial feedback within two weeks”.
- After positive feedback there is “an initial online interview with your direct supervisor”.
- If that goes well, an on-site interview “with your lead and a member of the People & Culture team”. It can be remote if you live far away.
- Before the on-site interview you get “a short task” to prepare at home and present.
- If that stage goes well, they get back to you “within the following days”.
