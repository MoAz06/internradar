## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply on the job posting page “from 16 December 2026 to 5 January 2027”.
- Your application must include a CV, your latest academic records and a cover letter that answers the questions in the job advert for your track.
- If selected, you get a first interview with the programme manager. If not, you are told by email.
- Next is the virtual Graduate Recruitment Centre: “a comprehensive assessment based on a business case that simulates a real-life scenario”.
- Remaining candidates get “a second and final interview”.
- You can apply to only one track: “Please note that you can only apply for 1 programme.”
