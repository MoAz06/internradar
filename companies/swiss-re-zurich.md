## Process, as the company describes it

- Apply online; “Only applications submitted electronically through our career website will be considered.”
- Online ability assessment(s): they “must be completed within 72 hours” after you get the email with the link, which comes shortly after you apply.
- Talent acquisition reviews all applications; if you pass the first stage “you might expect a short call” about your skills, experience and motivation.
- Selected candidates are invited to an assessment centre. The FAQ says “All assessment centres are now virtual”.
- For the 2027 Zurich graduate roles the postings say Swiss Re aims to give an update “by 15 January 2027 at the latest” and holds the assessment centre on 17, 18 or 19 February 2027, with an informal networking dinner the evening before.
- The FAQ advises focusing on “two or three positions” and writing a cover letter that explains your motivation.
