## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online with the “Jetzt bewerben” button and get a confirmation email.
- If your profile fits, you are invited to interviews: “ein bis drei Interviews”, virtual, by phone or in person, and at least one on site.
- After the interviews you get a contract offer.
- Postings are open as long as they are visible on the job board.
- A voluntary internship lasts up to 3 months; a mandatory one follows your university’s rules. R&S advises applying three to six months before your start date.
- You must be enrolled for the whole internship.
