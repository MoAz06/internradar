## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply through the Volkswagen Group job portal with a candidate profile and your CV; a cover letter is recommended.
- You get a confirmation email, and the recruiting team forwards your application to the business unit.
- Depending on the position, there may be a technical assessment, either with the partner Futurepath or online.
- If that goes well, you have a technical interview with the department, then an HR interview.
- Interviews are always held online, and you get feedback within 48 hours of the HR interview.
- CARIAD says the process “may differ slightly” for some positions, including students.

## Re-apply rule

If you are not successful, CARIAD says “You’re welcome to try again”; no waiting period is named.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Pay: interns get “Remuneration: €13.90 per hour”, for 35 hours a week.
- Visa: non-EU citizens need a work permit.
- Not stated: relocation.
