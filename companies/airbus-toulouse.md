## Process, as the company describes it

- “Applications are open from September to December for 4 to 6-month placements. For summer placements of 4 to 8 weeks, applications open in January.”
- After your application is assessed, a “video interview that will be reviewed by a recruiter. Then, you will have an interview with your future manager.”
- You must be “a French/EU citizen or have authorization to live and work in France with an appropriate Visa”, and your university must be willing to sign the Convention de Stage.
- Several postings need a French security clearance, and two ask for French nationality.
- The ATR postings (ATR is a separate company that posts on the Airbus job board) have two steps: a CV review, then an interview with the manager.
- The global FAQ says “Some roles may include technical tests, case studies or online assessments.”
