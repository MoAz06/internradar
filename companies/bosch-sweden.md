## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online on the Bosch job board, with a CV upload, your LinkedIn or Indeed profile, or by hand; a cover letter is optional.
- You get a confirmation mail within minutes with a link to your candidate profile, where you can follow the status.
- If your application is picked, Bosch invites you for an interview; for very sensitive areas “kan en bakgrundskontroll komma att krävas” (a background check may be needed).
- Both Lund theses are for two students, working on site at the Lund office.
