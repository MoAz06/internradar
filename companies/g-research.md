## Process, as the company describes it

- Engineering internships: online application, then a HackerRank online assessment, then a technical screening call with two engineers, then an onsite assessment centre at the London office, then a senior manager interview.
- The graduates page gives a shorter engineering intern version: apply, online skills test, assessment centre, taking “one to two weeks” in total.
- Quant research internships: apply online, an online quantitative aptitude test, technical interviews, then a final interview with a functional lead.
- Research internships ask you to apply to one role only; G-Research considers you for all research internships.
- Quantitative Researcher (postgraduate): an online quant quiz (general or ML), then usually four one-hour technical interviews (two if your profile is ML), then leadership interviews. You get a status update within one week of applying.
- Graduate ML Engineer: a technical interview or remote test, a behavioural interview, further technical interviews, then management interviews.
