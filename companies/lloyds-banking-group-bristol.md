## Process, as the company describes it

- For the Software Engineer, Data Science & AI and Cyber Security Engineer schemes there is no CV or cover letter: “A CV or covering letter is not required.”
- Software Engineer and Cyber Security Engineer applicants do an online assessment: “The total time allocated for this assessment is 130 minutes.” Data Science & AI applicants get 120 minutes.
- Next is a Job Insight Assessment with video, written and preference questions.
- The final stage is a virtual assessment day with “a one-to-one interview, a group exercise, a technical exercise” and pre-work.
- Both the application form and the online assessment must be done before the closing date.

## Re-apply rule

The graduate FAQ says Lloyds accepts “one application per candidate” per year, and failed online assessments cannot be retaken.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Graduate schemes: £48,500 on the job board.
- Visa: “We cannot sponsor visas.”

## Notes

- You pick one office when you apply and stay based there for the scheme, with at least two days a week in that office.
