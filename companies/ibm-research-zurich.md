## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply with your CV. Most postings ask for “contact information for two or three references” and invite an optional short video about yourself.
- The Agent-Centric Data Systems posting asks instead for a CV, “a brief statement of interest (maximum one page), and your academic transcript.”
- Most 2026 postings say that after a screening of your documents, candidates are “contacted for a first technical discussion on their experience, background, and motivations, followed by coding interview and an AI/ML interview”.
- They add: “Further selection steps might be added based on candidates skills and project needs.”
- The PDE posting says: “Shortlisted candidates can expect one or more technical discussions, a coding interview, and a conversation about project fit.”
- The two 2025 postings (GPU data structures, Docling) do not describe the interview steps.
