## Process, as the company describes it

- The Talent Acquisition team reviews your resume.
- A 30 minute video or intro call with a TA Specialist about the position and your qualifications.
- Separate video interviews with the hiring manager and several cross-functional team members.
- A final conversation with the hiring manager and/or team members.
- Offer. Mendix adds: “Process may vary depending on role”.
