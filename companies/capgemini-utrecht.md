## Process, as the company describes it

- Applying takes 3 to 5 minutes: your CV, and a motivation letter if you want.
- Interviews by phone, video or at the office. They can include behavioural, cultural and technical tests, plus an identity and background check.
- Then the answer from the recruitment team.
- For a thesis internship you pick your top 3 from a list of assignments after you apply.

## Re-apply rule

You can apply to other roles after a rejection: “Zeker! We zijn altijd op zoek naar nieuw talent.” (Certainly! We are always looking for new talent.)

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Internships: “Een stagevergoeding van € 500 bruto per maand” (€500 gross a month).
- Young Professional Tracks: a starting salary of €3,730 a month, including a €750 mobility allowance and holiday pay.

## Notes

- The Young Professional Tracks are run by Sogeti, part of Capgemini.
