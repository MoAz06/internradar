## Process, as the company describes it

- Internships: postings ask for an online application with CV, certificates, a current enrolment certificate with your semester, and if relevant proof of a mandatory internship and of your standard study period. Interview steps are not described.
- Internship vacancies are “generally advertised on our job portal two to six months before the start of the internship.”
- Inspire graduate programme: online application, then an online assessment with “various test modules”, then a short video interview.
- The best candidates go to the Inspire Day, a recruiting event with three exercises; feedback comes after it.
- Each Inspire application phase lasts three weeks; no phase is open now.

## Re-apply rule

Inspire: if you are rejected after the Inspire Day, “you can apply again after one year.” Internships: Not stated.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Internships: “Remuneration depends on the type of internship and on the regulations in the respective country”, no figure given.
- Housing: in the Stuttgart area (not Sindelfingen) your advisor can arrange a room in the company dormitory if there is capacity; you pay part of the cost.
- Inspire: in Germany a permanent contract based on 35 hours from the start; salary not stated.
- Visa: applicants from outside the EEA “require a residence permit and, if applicable, a work permit”. No sponsorship statement.
