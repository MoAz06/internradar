## Process, as the company describes it

- Internships: “the selection is made directly in the respective department” and “Neither an assessment centre nor an online test await you here.”
- For internships the interview “will only take place with the respective department”. It can be “on-site, by phone or via Teams, depending on where you live.”
- Departments “have up to four weeks to carefully screen all incoming applications”, and the result shows in the applicant system.
- AcceleratiON trainee programme: after applying you get “the invitation to an online test via email”.
- If you make it to the next round, there is a video interview, then an assessment centre in a hybrid format. The page says the online assessment centre and the on-site interviews both take place in November.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Interns: “attractive remuneration, depending on the location and form of employment”, no figure given. Postings say “Attractive & fair compensation.”
- Compensation details are “discussed during the job interview.”
- Housing: “Apartments for students (subject to availability & only at the Munich location).”
- AcceleratiON: permanent contract; “The remuneration is based on the applicable collective agreements.”
- Visa: “Your nationality does not play a role”, but “the appropriate work permit” must be available by the start. No sponsorship statement.
