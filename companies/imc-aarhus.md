## Process, as the company describes it

- IMC's process in Europe has five steps: application, an (online) assessment, first-round interviews, final-round interviews and an offer.
- Tech interviews cover data structures and algorithms, memory and computer architecture, and concurrency.
- You may apply to several roles, but “each role has its own selection process”.
- IMC aims to give feedback “within a couple of days.”
- The Aarhus office is new: “IMC is establishing a new global energy trading business in Aarhus, Denmark”.
