## Process, as the company describes it

- You may apply to up to four business and location combinations in a recruiting year: “Any additional application will be auto withdrawn.” “A single applicant should not create multiple email addresses to apply to additional opportunities.”
- If selected, you record a HireVue video interview.
- “Engineering applicants will be asked to complete a technical assessment.”
- The last round is a Superday with additional interviews.
- The roles in The Hague are off-cycle internships: “Our Off-Cycle Program varies in length based on program/university for undergraduate students.” Start dates and deadlines are not stated.
