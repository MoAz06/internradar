## Process, as the company describes it

- Internships are voluntary for 1 to 3 months or mandatory for 2 to 6 months, at 35 hours a week; working students do up to 18 hours a week.
- You apply online with your CV or a social profile; a recruiter reviews it, then you are invited to an interview, which “kann je nach Art der Rolle ein mehrstufiger Prozess sein” (can take several steps).
- Recruiters can also recommend you for other roles that fit your background.
- Thales's German sites include Ditzingen near Stuttgart, Hamburg, Kiel, Munich, Ulm and Berlin.
