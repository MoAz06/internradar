## Process, as the company describes it

- You apply online using your CV or social profile.
- A recruiter assesses your application against the requirements of the role.
- You are invited for a formal interview with the recruiter and/or hiring manager. “This may be a multi-stage process depending on the nature of the role.”
- After a successful interview process you get a formal offer.
- For the Sensor Platform assignments, you name your chosen assignment in your motivation letter.
