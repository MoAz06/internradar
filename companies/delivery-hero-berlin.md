## Process, as the company describes it

- Step 1 is a 30-minute recruiter interview about you, your motivation and the company principles.
- Step 2 is a 60-minute coding interview with an engineer: technical topics plus “a live coding exercise through coderpad.io”.
- Step 3 is a Bar Raiser interview with another engineer on concepts like environment setup, version control and debugging.
- Step 4 is a 60-minute hiring manager interview with a senior engineer, with more coding and technical questions.
- The FAQ says the recruiting team gets back to you within a maximum of 2 weeks after you apply.
