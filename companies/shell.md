## Process, as the company describes it

- Stage one is a Virtual Job Tryout followed by an On-Demand Interview, which together “will take approximately 40 minutes”.
- You must finish the On-Demand Interview “within 7 days from receiving the invitation”.
- Stage two is a virtual case study session “with two Shell employees”. Graduate Programme applicants also have a virtual interview with the same two people.
- After stage two you could be offered a place in the Graduate Programme or the Assessed Internship, “depending on currently available opportunities”.
- Assessed interns are assessed during the internship and could get a full-time offer “without the need for a further interview”.
- For the internship, your CV must be in English and you include a motivation letter.

## Re-apply rule

After a Graduate Programme application, any further application within 10 months is “auto-progressed, based on the outcome of the first application”. If the first one was unsuccessful, the new one is “automatically regretted”.

## Pay, relocation, visa

- Graduates: “a competitive starting salary and annual performance-related salary increases”, no figure given.
- Not stated for the Assessed Internship.
- Visa: not mentioned. Graduates must “Live in the Netherlands”; interns “must be lawfully authorized to work” in the country they apply in.
