## Re-apply rule

Optiver has a global re-apply policy for intern and graduate roles. If you took an online assessment or interviewed for a technology graduate or internship role at any Optiver office in the past eight months, you have to wait out those eight months before applying again. (Source: the posting, checked 2026-11-16.)
