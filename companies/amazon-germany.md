## Process, as the company describes it

- SDE internships: “The SDE Online Assessment (OA) is the first step in the hiring process”. For internships it “takes about 90 minutes”: coding assessment (about 70 min), work styles assessment (about 15 min) and a feedback survey.
- In the coding assessment you may use public online resources; “Your browser usage is logged.”
- Applied Science interns take an OA with a coding assessment and a work styles assessment (about 70 minutes for the coding part).
- Applied Scientist PhD internship: one application, then you are matched with teams; “your recruiter will confirm the preferences of the team you're matched with prior to interviewing.”
- Interview rounds after the OA are not described on the pages checked.
